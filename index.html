Minecraft Wiki

Minecraft is a popular sandbox game where players can explore, build, craft, and survive in a world made of blocks.

Players can collect resources, create tools, build houses, and fight different creatures.

The game has different modes such as Survival and Creative. Players can also explore different biomes, caves, villages, and dimensions like the Nether and the End.

Minecraft Worlds

Minecraft features three unique dimensions to explore: the Overworld, the Nether, and the End. Each world has its own biomes, structures, resources, and dangerous creatures.

Overworld

The Overworld is the main world where players start their journey. It has diverse biomes, including forests, deserts, oceans, and mountains. Players can find villages, temples, and strongholds in this world.

Nether

The Nether is a dangerous dimension filled with lava, fire, and hostile mobs. Players can find unique resources like Nether Quartz and Glowstone. The Nether is also home to fortresses and bastions.

The End

The End is a mysterious dimension where players can find the Ender Dragon, the final boss of the game. It consists of floating islands and is home to End Cities and Shulkers. Players can also find valuable resources like End Stone and Elytra.
