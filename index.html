Minecraft Wiki

Minecraft is a popular sandbox game where players can explore, build, craft, and survive in a world made of blocks.

Players can collect resources, create tools, build houses, and fight different creatures.

Minecraft gives players the freedom to create almost anything they can imagine.

Minecraft Worlds

Minecraft has three main dimensions, and each one has a different environment.

Overworld

The Overworld is the main dimension where players start their adventure. It contains forests, deserts, oceans, mountains, villages and many other biomes.

Nether

The Nether is a dangerous dimension filled with lava, fire and hostile mobs. Players can find Nether Quartz, Glowstone, Fortresses and Bastions.

The End

The End is a mysterious dimension where players can fight the Ender Dragon. It contains End Cities, Shulkers and the valuable Elytra.

Minecraft Game Modes

Survival Mode

In Survival mode, players must gather resources, manage hunger, and survive against hostile mobs.

Creative Mode

In Creative mode, players have unlimited resources and can fly. They can build and create without worrying about survival.

Adventure Mode

Adventure mode is designed for players to explore custom maps and adventures created by others. Players cannot break blocks without the right tools.

Hardcore Mode

In Hardcore mode, players have only one life. If they die, they cannot respawn and must start over.
